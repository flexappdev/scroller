# Top 100 Cinematic Scenes

The standalone gallery runs at `/top100/cinema`; the collection index is `/top100`.
Its static source, data and 100 local images live in `public/top100/cinema/`.
The route rewrite in `next.config.ts` serves its HTML at the clean app URL.

The source package contained 90 complete images and 10 empty files. The empty
slots (12, 16, 17, 19–22, 36, 44 and 46) were filled with new individual
16:9 images based on their corresponding scene descriptions. Each image is
stored as WebP under its original numbered filename.
