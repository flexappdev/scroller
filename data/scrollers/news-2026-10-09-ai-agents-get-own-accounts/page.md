# AI Agents Get Their Own Accounts

**Snapshot:** 9 October 2026 · Europe/London

## Why this is today's lead

At Gemini at Work 2026 on 8 October, Google Cloud announced a universal workplace agent that can run tasks lasting hours or days. Set up as a coworker, it can receive its own Workspace account, email address, calendar and Drive. It follows Microsoft's Copilot Autopilot and OpenAI's Dots within about two weeks, so every major platform now offers a persistent agent with its own identity. The same week, Pwn2Own Ireland showed that agent tooling such as OpenAI Codex and LiteLLM is a live attack surface.

## What is verified

- The agent's capabilities, Workspace identity, memory types, governance controls, integrations and cost controls, as reported by VentureBeat (source 1).
- Gemini Enterprise adoption figures (Fortune 100, BNP Paribas, SOMPO) and rival launches from Microsoft, OpenAI, Anthropic and SpaceXAI (source 1).
- Pwn2Own day-one totals and the Codex, LiteLLM and Oracle results (source 2).

## What remains uncertain

- Pricing, availability date, licensing and admin controls for the universal agent.
- How reliable the agent is on long, cross-application tasks; no independent benchmarks have been published.
- The adoption figures describe Gemini Enterprise, not the new agent.
