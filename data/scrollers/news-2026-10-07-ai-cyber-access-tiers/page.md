# AI Cyber Power Gets Gatekept

**Snapshot:** 7 October 2026 · Europe/London

## Why this is today's lead

On 6 October 2026 Anthropic turned its Cyber Verification Program into three access tiers (Defense, Red Team and Specialized) that decide how much offensive-security help Claude gives a verified organisation. Its own tests show the public model blocking every cyber task, Defense Access blocking 46 of 50 and Red Team Access blocking none. Project Glasswing partners moved into the Specialized tier, with Anthropic reporting 129,000 verified vulnerabilities found by partners between April and July.

The same week, Shinhan, KB Kookmin and Hana banks in South Korea disclosed customer data leaks, with analysts finding traces of the ARTEX AI penetration-testing tool on a server linked to the Shinhan attack. Labs are rationing AI hacking power by identity just as attackers show they can get capable tools elsewhere.

## What is verified

- Anthropic's three tiers, eligibility, review times, real-time blocks and cloud availability (source 1).
- CyScenarioBench results on Claude Opus 5.5: all blocked when generally available, 46 of 50 blocked under Defense Access, 0 blocked and 34 of 50 completed under Red Team Access (source 1).
- Glasswing partners found 129,000 verified vulnerabilities (April to July); Anthropic's open-source scanning found 5,500 more (April to October); over 33,000 rated critical or high (source 1).
- Bank breach counts: Shinhan about 25,000 customers, KB Kookmin 119, Hana 89, BNK 11 outsourced staff records (sources 2 and 3).
- ARTEX AI traces on a Shinhan-linked server, not officially confirmed (source 2).

## What remains uncertain

- Whether AI tools were used in each Korean breach; officials had not confirmed ARTEX AI.
- Dimon's "10-fold" and President Lee's quotes are reported second-hand through Fox News' live coverage.
- How many organisations will qualify for Specialized Access, and on what terms.
