## Verified now

- The litigation is in the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of New York.
- OpenAI and Microsoft face claims brought by The New York Times and other copyright owners.
- The central dispute includes whether copying copyrighted works for AI training is protected by fair use.
- The U.S. government has filed in support of OpenAI's broad fair-use position.
- U.S. Copyright Office analysis says AI-training fair use is fact-specific: some uses may qualify and some may not.

## Disputed / not yet decided

- Whether any ruling will be broad or tightly limited to the facts.
- Whether the dispute will proceed to appeal after the next ruling.
