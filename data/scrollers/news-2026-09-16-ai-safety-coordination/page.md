# AI Rivals Begin Coordinating on Safety

**Snapshot:** 16 September 2026 · Europe/London.

## Winning headline

OpenAI says it has been working with Anthropic and Google DeepMind on AI-safety measures for several weeks, while the industry and policymakers debate how far competitors can coordinate, how independent verification should work, and when safety concerns should actually slow frontier development.

## Why this story won

This was selected as the ScrollAI Daily lead because it combines the highest-priority category (frontier AI) with a potentially structural shift: direct safety coordination among major competing labs. It is more consequential for the AI ecosystem than a routine model or product launch because it could affect evaluation standards, launch gates, regulation and cross-company incident handling.

## What is verified

- Chris Lehane, OpenAI's global policy chief, is reported as saying OpenAI has been working with Anthropic and Google DeepMind on AI-safety measures for several weeks.
- Lehane said OpenAI does not believe a special antitrust waiver is necessary for safety coordination.
- OpenAI's September 9 policy statement explicitly calls for mandatory capability-based national AI-safety requirements and says it wants to work with other labs on industry-led standards.
- OpenAI says those industry standards should complement, not replace, mandatory safeguards and democratic oversight.
- OpenAI has also backed independent safety assessment, auditor standards, serious-incident reporting and international coordination.

## What remains disputed or unknown

- Reuters said it had not independently verified the Bloomberg account of the cross-company discussions.
- No binding joint framework, treaty or public shared threshold has yet been announced by the three companies.
- The scope of the discussions, decision rights, enforcement mechanism and degree of independent oversight are still unclear.
- Antitrust authorities have not publicly established a dedicated exemption or special regime for this coordination.
- It remains unknown whether the talks will directly alter model-release timing or produce enforceable pause criteria.
