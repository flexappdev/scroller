## Verified facts

- Reuters reported on 11 September 2026 that the UAE is revising plans for a 5GW AI data-center project after the Iran war changed the physical threat environment.
- Reuters says the original 10-square-mile Abu Dhabi campus will likely become a network of data centers spread across the UAE.
- Officials are considering underground construction, air-defence systems, electronic jamming, blast-resistant concrete, and additional backup power and cooling.
- Reuters says the first 1GW Stargate UAE phase is expected to continue, with protective modifications.
- OpenAI and G42 previously announced a 1GW Stargate UAE cluster, with the first 200MW expected to go live in 2026.
- G42’s original announcement describes Stargate UAE as part of a 5GW UAE–U.S. AI Campus and names OpenAI, Oracle, Nvidia, Cisco and SoftBank as partners.
- Reuters reports that two AWS data centers in the UAE and one in Bahrain were damaged in Iranian attacks in March.
- G42 told Reuters work is progressing as planned while project specifics remain subject to continuous review for security, resilience and operational standards.
