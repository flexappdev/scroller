# Cheaper Models, Visual Answers

**Snapshot:** 8 October 2026 · Europe/London

## Why this is today's lead

On 7 October 2026 Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5, which it says costs about 75% less than Haiku 4.5 on average while scoring far higher on office, computer-use and coding benchmarks. The same day OpenAI began rolling GPT-6 out to every ChatGPT tier, together with Intelligent UI, which puts interactive charts, calculators and diagrams inside answers. Developers get cheap capability and consumers get the newest model, both on the same day.
