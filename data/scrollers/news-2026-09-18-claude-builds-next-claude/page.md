# Claude Is Helping Build the Next Claude

**ScrollAI Daily — 18 September 2026 · Europe/London**

## Why this is today's lead

Anthropic has published unusually concrete measurements of how much frontier-model research is now performed by AI itself. The company says Claude **leads 26% of its measured AI R&D work**, while more than **90%** is at the level of AI collaboration or higher. Reuters and AP both reported the disclosure, emphasizing that Claude remains under human supervision rather than autonomously building a successor.

For ScrollAI, the durable significance is that the debate about recursive self-improvement now has an operational metric that can be tracked over time.

## Source-checked facts

- Claude is **not fully autonomous** for any measured subset of Anthropic's AI R&D.
- Claude **leads 26%** of measured AI R&D work as of August 2026.
- More than **90%** of measured work is at AI-collaboration level or higher.
- Anthropic reports roughly **30,000 agents** active at any one time on its most-used internal research and engineering platform.
- It says **100%** of covered agent actions pass through an online monitor.
- Across more than a billion August decisions, about **0.002%** were blocked, roughly **1 in 47,000**.
- About **6%** of sampled AI-R&D compute was categorized as safety work and about **12%** for AI-driven AI R&D.
- Anthropic proposes public reporting plus independent third-party verification.

## Caveats

These are company-produced measurements using Anthropic's methodology and are not yet directly comparable with other frontier labs. The 26% figure does **not** mean Claude autonomously designs, trains, evaluates and deploys its own successor. Trend extrapolations are not forecasts.
