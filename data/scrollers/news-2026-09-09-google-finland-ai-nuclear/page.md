## Verified now

- Google announced €13 billion of Finland investment across 2027–2028.
- Google calls it its largest single investment in Europe.
- The plan includes data centers and supporting infrastructure in Hamina, Kajaani, Muhos and Vaala.
- Google and Fortum signed a 22-year nuclear PPA tied to Loviisa’s life extension.
- The PPA can ramp to up to 50% of the plant’s capacity during 2030–2049.
- Google has contracted for a new 94 MW battery system near Kajaani.
- Google says new onshore wind capacity is also part of the Finnish energy plan.
- Fortum says Loviisa currently provides about 10% of Finland’s electricity.
